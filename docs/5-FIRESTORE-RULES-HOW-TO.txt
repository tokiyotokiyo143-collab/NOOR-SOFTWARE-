How to publish (owner does this, 2 minutes, safe to do after testing):
1. console.firebase.google.com > project hale-thought-sk4jk > Firestore Database > Rules.
2. Copy the old rules somewhere first (backup).
3. Paste firestore.rules, press Publish.
4. Open the app as owner: the "Cloud sync blocked" banner should go. Log in as a staff user: bills/stock work, but cost price and profit must stay hidden, and delete must fail.
5. If anything breaks, paste the old rules back.
